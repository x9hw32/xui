iOS26Anim 0.1.0+rajok.1

by Stealth2115

iOS 26-style app opening and closing animations for iPhone 8 on iOS 16. Upstream source by Stealth2115; rootless and rootful packages built by rajok. The original animation code and tuning are preserved. This is an approximation of iOS 26 animations, not its Metal rendering.

Upstream tuning: 1.50-second opening/closing duration, spring response 0.90, damping 0.60 and blur alpha 0.95. No Settings pane; tuning requires rebuilding.

Анимации открытия и закрытия приложений в стиле iOS 26. Сборки rootless и rootful для iPhone 8 на iOS 16. Автор исходников — Stealth2115, сборка пакетов — rajok. На устройстве пока не проверено.

Built for ARM64 iPhone 8; device testing pending.
